### Team execution and automatic recovery

Approved invariants, not new Product Owner questions:

- Authored fixed fragments and actual member count are independent. Four fragments allocate 2+2, 2+1+1, 1+1+1+1 or four one-fragment and two no-fragment assignments for six members. Allocation is persisted, independent of finish order; rotate fairly where feasible. No fabricated fragments/roles. No-fragment members still complete required individual activities before recovery. Personal completion satisfies a Team member activity only where an explicit reviewed binding declares that relationship; neither state implies the other by default.
- Persist availability and server deadline exactly five minutes later, individual activity completion, assignment/disclosure, accepted contributions, collective validation, recovery/reassignment and progression as separate facts. Timer starts when the Team Challenge becomes available to the team, never from a client clock, page mount or reconnect. Exact availability event and pause behavior are remaining D2 details.
- Before recovery: all active members' required individual activities completed, all required fragments legitimately available and backend-valid collective solution. After deadline, any unfinished required activity automatically activates recovery; those unfinished originals cease blocking. Still require all fragments earned and a valid collective solution. No vote or Organizer approval. Disconnect neither completes an activity nor removes a member.
- Missing fragments reassign to eligible members who completed their original required activities. Each fragment is disclosed only after the assigned appropriate recovery activity succeeds. Preserve earned fragments/contributions/scores; no automatic secret/solution reveal. A supported recovery mechanic must permit one participating member to recover; its actual definition is D5, not an invented generic answer bypass.
- Notify the Team without naming/blaming unfinished members. An authorized late original may complete while the Team remains at the checkpoint, with no duplicate disclosure or points. After advance, close unfinished originals and reject their mutation. Returning members restore state without rollback; cancellation of racing reassignments is D2 policy bounded by once-only disclosure.
- Persist time/revisions; evaluate deadline under the same transactional guard on every Team operation and resume. A small due-state worker may materialize activation/notifications for idle Teams; no WebSocket service or second engine. Reads must observe the effective deadline state even if that worker is delayed. Original/recovery completion at the deadline, reassignment and final submission serialize against compatible membership/allocation locks. Stale allocation revision is 409 and reload, never stale unlock.
- Exactly **+100 once to the team** for a successful Team Challenge, normal or recovery, independent of size; **0 if unfinished**. Individual/recovery activity completion and fragment disclosure have no partial/additional Team Challenge points. Recovery incurs no penalty and never clears previously earned scores. HELP/PANIC is never auto-resolved by recovery.

### Scoring and terminal settlement

One Team award identity per `(Hunt, execution digest, checkpoint, Team challenge, Team)` protects all retries, members and normal/recovery paths. A key per submitting member alone is insufficient. A winning collective transaction records completion, +100 event and unlock/terminal result atomically; a different-key duplicate returns existing success with zero new award. Three successful Team Challenges total 300; seven successful configured Team Challenges total 700. FinishPoint's configured Team Challenge uses this same event; it is not a second +100 terminal award.

Persist Personal results separately from Team activity completion and Team points. Do not sum members' Personal totals into Team Challenge score. Snapshot starting balance, Personal penalties/first-attempt bonus, terminal puzzle +250 and mission +100 are legacy data; applicability/beneficiary and overlap with the approved Team rule require D3 resolution. No new runtime may award these merely because the fields exist. Once separately approved, each distinct award category needs its own eligibility predicate and unique event identity; it cannot duplicate a Team success under a different label. Progress/result DTOs separate Team Challenge points from unresolved Personal/other categories and do not expose an invented combined leaderboard. Reset/Passport eligibility remains D3; preserve authoritative earned history.

## Proposed API and trust boundary

Reuse `/api/hunts/{id}`. Existing `GET /api/hunt-access/{code}` is public metadata only; `GET /api/me/hunt-contexts` requires bearer authentication; Hunt detail remains Organizer/Supervisor access, not a Participant challenge source. Existing Creator save/version/submit and Admin review endpoints continue to return full definitions only under their current authorization.

Proposed operations, all authenticated with existing `requireAuth`, all unavailable today:

| Method/path | Proposed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/hunts/{id}/checkpoints/{checkpointId}/challenges/{classification}` | Deliver accessible Personal or Team slot; return opaque challenge ID and pinned version plus allowlisted interaction and member-specific information |
| `POST .../challenges/{classification}/attempts` | Strict answer submission with required `Idempotency-Key`; no score/correctness/participant/Team ID from client |
| `POST .../challenges/{classification}/hints` | Idempotently release only the next authorized hint after policy checks; never arbitrary hint index or initial hints array |
| `GET /api/hunts/{id}/progress` | Caller-scoped persisted subject completion, separate score categories and checkpoint state for reload/resume/results (including authorized reads after global finish); no other members' clues or responses |

`checkpointId` is an opaque server-resolved identifier scoped to the Hunt snapshot, including terminal; it is not an array index supplied by FE. `classification` is `personal|team`. Allocate/map checkpoint identity without modifying snapshot JSON. Bind opaque challenge identity to Hunt, checkpoint, slot and exact custom UUID/owner (or Legacy adapter identity). Reuse #95 checkpoint UUIDs and the #96 execution digest; only challenge/activity identity allocation remains new. No ephemeral per-request identities. Check Hunt status, active account, enrollment, Team membership for Team operations, visit/access grant, checkpoint order, suspension and stage requirements for every operation. A role alone is insufficient. Participants who are also organizers still receive the safe projection on these routes.

Response allowlist: `challengeId`, `templateVersion`, `type`, `classification`, `schemaVersion`, `title`, `instructions`, mechanic-specific `interaction`, `playerInformation`, completion status, and the closed Team-only `teamExecution` state/revisions. Nested objects are closed. Never spread a checkpoint, `interaction` object or Creator definition into a DTO. For Personal mechanics the exact allowlisted interaction fields are those in the matrix; no answer object, correct ID, answer mappings, hints, solution, feedback, owner metadata or raw snapshot. Option IDs are legitimate public item references; never mark which ID is correct. Wrong guesses are not returned as expected answers. Narrative/instruction content still needs human approval: an allowlist cannot detect an author writing a solution in a title.

For Distributed Information return public `options` and only earned, authorized `playerInformation.facts`, not the full facts collection, player count indexes or other members' assignment map. After earned disclosure authorization, Shared Final Key returns `orderingInstructions` plus only the caller's authorized `playerInformation.contributions`. A submitted combined key may contain information players have intentionally shared; no response exposes the stored order/code. Remaining per-mechanic disclosure/activity bindings require D5; adaptive allocation and earned disclosure themselves are approved. No WebSocket/event infrastructure is introduced; resume/poll uses the same authenticated DTOs.

Initial delivery includes no assistance or feedback. Proposed hints operation returns only `{hint, ordinal}` after explicit authorization, persisted release and any configured score mutation in one transaction. Repeating the key returns the same hint without another penalty. No solution endpoint is proposed until D4 resolves release semantics; omit solutions everywhere for now. Do not derive a new penalty from number of attempts or invent hint costs. HTTP `Cache-Control: no-store` is required for private gameplay responses. Never log answer bodies, private clues, tokens or expected definitions; audit scoped IDs/outcomes only.

Strict error envelope `{error:string}`: 400 malformed/unknown fields, 401 invalid account/token, 404 inaccessible Hunt/checkpoint/challenge/membership (uniform concealment), 409 invalid Hunt state/progression/suspension/version/idempotency conflict, 422 unsupported gameplay/policy unresolved, 429 attempt budget (`Retry-After`), 500 generic internal error. Valid but incorrect answer: 200 with `correct:false`, authoritative completion/score. Feedback cannot include expected answer definitions. Recheck authorization even for a cached idempotency result. Gameplay budgets must use authenticated Hunt/participant scope and Team aggregate where appropriate; rate/attempt limits require D4 and operator configuration, not reuse login budgets by accident.

## Transaction, persistence and version proposal

No migration is included. Reuse `hunts`, `hunt_participants`, `teams`, `team_members`, `hunt_checkpoint_identities`, `participant_checkpoint_access`, immutable `template_snapshot` and frozen `checkpoint_radius_configuration`. Extend access/grant persistence rather than duplicate it. Add only missing subject challenge/activity state, attempts/idempotency, stable fragment assignments/recovery and authoritative score/result events. Exact DDL belongs to the bounded implementation slices after applicable decisions clear; no mutable exercise library or replacement engine.

Required invariants for those tables:

1. Bind every state/attempt to Hunt and pinned snapshot version, checkpoint, classification and challenge identity. Composite FKs must prohibit cross-Hunt participant/Team references; use existing composite membership model. Validate stored custom UUID and owner against the Hunt snapshot, never a client version or latest Creator version.
2. Persist a unique completion per `(hunt, snapshot, checkpoint, classification, subject)`; subject is authenticated HuntParticipant for Personal, current Team for Team. Use separate/checked subject fields rather than nullable uniqueness that permits duplicates. Store Team membership epoch/assignment identity if D2 permits roster changes.
3. Within one transaction serialize a consistent lock order on Hunt execution/suspension state, checkpoint and subject challenge state. Resolve membership and access again under the transaction. Membership/suspension writers must use compatible locking. Insert attempt, validate, record completion and update score once before commit; rollback all on failure. Concurrent wrong attempts must also serialize first-attempt eligibility and penalties.
4. Idempotency is scoped to authenticated participant, Hunt, operation and challenge; same key and canonical request hash returns the original result after access recheck; same key/different data is 409. Unique keys and completion/award constraints protect different-key replay and simultaneous Team submissions. Retry after network loss cannot repeat penalties/awards. Define retention at least through Hunt settlement; don't delete attempt keys while replay can score. A fresh key for another wrong attempt follows D4, not automatic deduplication by answer text.
5. Stable assignments map stored item IDs to HuntParticipant UUIDs in the Team/Hunt/challenge scope. Persist once and return the same data on reload. Decide roster size/leave/rejoin policy before allocation. No client indexes, name parsing, round-robin on each GET or volatile roster order.
6. Completion and scoring are server outputs. Ignore no unknown field silently: reject `score`, `correct`, `completed`, `teamId`, `participantId`, `owner` and unexpected nested fields. Supplied templateVersion is a consistency precondition, never snapshot selection. New Template revision/copy IDs cannot answer an old Hunt.
7. Resume reads committed state. Team completion is shared and once-only; Personal progress remains per participant. FinishPoint checks configured compatible Personal and Team requirements plus all prior required checkpoints before subject success; it does not automatically close the global Hunt or award an unresolved mission bonus. Historical Signal seventh entry is interpreted without renumbering stored data; new terminal remains unnumbered. Empty/unconfigured slots need explicit completion policy, never Signal fallback.
8. Competition score reset cannot remove Passport history or permit re-earning the same durable award. No historical snapshot/state backfill from untrusted browser results. D3/D8 must decide Legacy transition and durable award identity.
